Remote Rift Privacy Policy

This policy covers the Remote Rift mobile app, developed by Tomasz Wyrowiński.

Desktop connection and game data

The mobile app uses local network discovery to find your Remote Rift Desktop companion. It uses the companion's network address and service information to connect. The companion sends League of Legends client status and matchmaking data to the phone, including lobby and queue status, role preferences, ready checks, and champion select activity. The phone sends your actions and selections back to the companion, which applies them through your signed-in League client. Riot Games handles gameplay and account data through its client and services. The mobile app does not ask for your Riot credentials or create a Remote Rift account.

The phone and desktop companion exchange this data directly using HTTP and WebSocket connections without transport encryption. Use a trusted private network for this connection. The mobile app uses matchmaking data for the active connection and does not send it to a Remote Rift cloud service.

Diagnostics, storage, and deletion

Diagnostic logging is off by default. If enabled, the mobile app stores logs in its app storage on your device. Logs include timestamps, the app version, a randomly generated session identifier, and events and outcomes related to app startup, discovery, connection, and matchmaking actions. The app applies retention limits of seven days and 2 MiB. You can turn off future logging or clear existing logs in Settings. Clearing the app's storage removes its local settings and logs.

The app does not automatically upload logs or send app data to advertising or analytics providers. If you choose to share a log, the app passes a copy to the app or recipient you select in your device's share sheet. Clearing Remote Rift's logs does not delete copies you already shared.

Privacy questions

Use the support email on Remote Rift's Google Play listing for private questions or deletion requests. For general questions, you can also use the Remote Rift issue tracker. Issues are public. Keep personal information and diagnostic logs out of public issues.
